# 🩺 MedClear AI - Clinical Plain-Language Simplifier

> **AI Patient Advocate & Health Literacy Translation Engine**  
> Developed for **Build With AI: Basics ($2,500 USD Prize Pool)** on Devpost.

---

## 🌟 The Problem
Over 80 million patients globally struggle to understand dense medical notes, discharge summaries, and lab findings. Complex terms like *dyslipidemia*, *dyspnea*, or *edema* cause unnecessary patient anxiety and missed treatment steps.

## 💡 The Solution
**MedClear AI** takes any clinical doctor note or discharge report and instantly:
1. **Translates Medical Jargon to Plain Language**: Injects understandable, patient-friendly definitions.
2. **Generates an Actionable Next-Steps Checklist**: Highlights urgent symptoms vs routine monitoring.
3. **Preserves 100% Privacy**: Operates fully offline with zero data leakage.

---

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

```bash
# Run verification tests
python3 test_simplifier.py -v

python3 simplifier.py
```
